Our Story

Built on a Commitment to Cleanliness

EPRISOL was founded with a single conviction: that every workspace deserves to be clean, safe, and professionally maintained. What began as a small team serving a handful of offices in Nairobi has grown into one of Kenya's most respected commercial and industrial cleaning companies.

Over the years, we have invested heavily in state-of-the-art equipment, rigorous staff training, and eco-friendly cleaning solutions — ensuring that every client receives a service that is not just clean, but truly exceptional.

Today, EPRISOL serves over 200 clients across Nairobi, Mombasa, Nakuru, Eldoret, and Kisumu, with a team of over 15 expert technicians who are trained, certified, and passionate about their craft.
